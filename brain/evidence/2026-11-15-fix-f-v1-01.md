## F-V1-01 (Mayor) — compensación release-seats agotada ya NO deja SEATS_RESERVED eterno

**Causa raíz (confirmada):** `SagaProcessManager` caso `step-failed` — la transición a
FAILED solo existía vía `seats-released`; el `step-failed` de la compensación caía en
`TryNoteFailureAsync` = false ("fallo ya anotado") y se ignoraba.

**Fix (enmienda D-0012):** `step-failed` con `step = release-seats` → transición CAS
directa `SEATS_RESERVED|PAYMENT_APPROVED → FAILED` con reason que declara la reserva
huérfana. NO re-emite la compensación. Duplicados → guard CAS → ignorados.

```
src/Services/Orders/TicketFlow.Orders.Core/Application/SagaProcessManager.cs -> HandleAsync case StepFailed (rama evt.Step == ReleaseSeats)
tests/TicketFlow.Orders.Tests/SagaProcessManagerTests.cs -> 3 tests F-V1-01 (terminal+huérfana, variante PAYMENT_APPROVED, duplicado ignorado)
tests/TicketFlow.Orders.Tests/SagaEndToEndTests.cs -> InjectedCatalogWorker + Persistent_failure_on_release_seats_ends_terminal_failed_with_explicit_orphan (repro e2e)
```
